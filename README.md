# aml-cft-risk-matrices
### Institutional AML/CFT Risk Assessment Matrices & Wolfsberg CBDDQ Alignment Models

Open-source templates, methodology handbooks, and scoring spreadsheets conforming to international supervisory baselines.

---

## Contents

- **`matrices/enterprise_aml_risk_scoring_matrix.csv`**: Standardized quantitative risk-scoring model across four essential risk pillars:
  1. Customer Risk (PEP, High-Net-Worth, Non-Resident, Corporate Structure)
  2. Geographic Risk (FATF Grey/Blacklist, High-Risk Remittance Corridors)
  3. Product & Service Risk (Cash transactions, Cross-border wire transfers, Virtual Asset gateways)
  4. Delivery Channel Risk (Non-face-to-face, Third-party agents)
- **Wolfsberg CBDDQ v1.4 Guidance**: Structured alignment checklist for correspondent banking due diligence.
